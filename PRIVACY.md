Arya is an open-source voice companion for Android. It has **no server and no user accounts**. It talks directly to
Google's Gemini API using **your own API key**.

## What Arya processes

| Data | Why | Where it goes | Kept? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Voice from the microphone | Only while a conversation is active (and, if you turn on "Hey Arya", briefly to detect the wake word) | Conversation audio is sent to Google's Gemini API. Wake-word detection uses Android's speech recognizer (on-device with an offline pack on Android 13+, otherwise Google's speech service). | Arya does not store recordings. Google handles data sent to Gemini under its own terms and your API key's settings. |
| Text you type and Arya's replies | The conversation | Sent to Google's Gemini API | The on-screen transcript is kept in memory only and disappears when the app process ends. |
| "Memories" you ask Arya to keep | Personalisation | Stored only on your phone; included in the instructions sent to Gemini during a conversation | Until you delete them in Settings. |
| Gemini API key | Authenticate to Google | Stored encrypted on your phone (Android Keystore); sent only to Google | Until you replace it or clear app data. |
| Contacts (optional) | Look up a number when you ask Arya to call someone | Read on the phone only. If several contacts match, their names are shared with Gemini so Arya can ask which one. | Not stored. |
| Installed-app names | To open an app you ask for | Read on the phone only | Not stored. |

Arya contains **no ads, no analytics and no tracking**.

## Permissions

Microphone (conversations and wake word), notifications and foreground service (the visible "Arya is listening / talking"
notification), alarms (set an alarm or timer in your clock app), contacts (optional, call by name), internet.

## Your choices

- Turn the wake word off, or stop a conversation, at any time.
- Delete memories in Settings. Clear the app's storage in Android settings to remove everything, including the API key.
- Reports: "Report a problem" and long-pressing a reply open your email app so you can review what is sent.

## Children

Arya is not directed to children and should not be used by anyone under the age allowed by the Gemini API terms.

## AI content

Replies are generated by AI and can be wrong or inappropriate. Do not rely on them for medical, legal or financial decisions.
